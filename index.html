SeaTools

Tools for the bridge and the engine room

Free browser apps for ship's crews. Nothing to install: open on a PC, tablet or phone. Once opened, they keep working offline.

Apps

Works offline

Open this page once with internet. After that all apps load from the device, even with no connection on board.

Your data stays on your device

What you enter in the apps is saved in this browser only and is never sent anywhere. The site only counts anonymous visits, without cookies. Use each app's export or print to keep a copy.

Add to your home screen

Give an app its own icon on any tablet or phone. Open the app first, then:

  • iPad / iPhone: in Safari tap Share, then “Add to Home Screen”.
  • Android: in Chrome tap the ⋮ menu, then “Add to Home screen” or “Install app”.
  • PC: in Chrome or Edge click the install icon in the address bar.
