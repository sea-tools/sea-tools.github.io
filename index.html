Apps

Works offline

Open this page once with internet. After that all apps load from the device, even with no connection on board.

Your data stays on your device

What you enter in the apps is saved in this browser only and is never sent anywhere. The site only counts anonymous visits, without cookies. Use each app's export or print to keep a copy.

Install one app at a time

Each app installs on its own, with its own icon and window, in the browser you are using on this device. In Chrome and Edge a card “Install …” appears inside the app. Otherwise open the app you want first (not this page), then:

  • iPad / iPhone: in Safari tap Share, then “Add to Home Screen”.
  • Android: in Chrome tap the ⋮ menu, then “Install app” or “Add to Home screen”.
  • PC / Mac, Edge: click the install icon at the right end of the address bar, or ⋮ → Apps → “Install this site as an app”.
  • PC / Mac, Chrome: click the install icon in the address bar, or ⋮ → Save and share → “Install page as app”.

Where is it afterwards? On a PC open it from the Start menu (type its name); right-click it there → Pin to taskbar, or drag the icon to the desktop for a shortcut. On a Mac use Launchpad. On a tablet or phone it is on the home screen.

To remove an installed app, uninstall it like any other app on your device.
