Privacy

What you enter in the apps is stored in your browser only, on the device you are using. It is never sent to this site or to anyone else. DP Footprint, Port Clearance, DP Logbook and Plot to Route can optionally keep their data also in a file that you choose on your own network drive, so computers on the same network can share it. That file is yours: it is never uploaded anywhere, and the apps do not use it unless you link it.

The site counts page opens and home-screen installs with GoatCounter, an anonymous counter that sets no cookies and collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no advertising and no tracking across other sites.

Clearing your browser's site data removes what the apps have saved. Use each app's export or print to keep a copy.

Methods and data sources

  • Sailings and geodesy: rhumb lines and geodesics on the WGS84 ellipsoid (Vincenty's method); great circles on the sphere, as in standard navigation practice.
  • Sun and Aries: computed after J. Meeus, Astronomical Algorithms. Against published almanac data the Sun agrees to about 0.2′.
  • Navigational stars: mean places derived from, and checked against, the celestial navigation data service of the U.S. Naval Observatory. Agreement is about 0.02′.
  • Ship, tide and coastal tools: standard textbook formulae. Hydrostatic values and tidal data are entered by you from the ship's own documents and official tide tables.
